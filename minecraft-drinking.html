Minecraft Drinking Game

Spin two wheels at the start of every Minecraft day. One gives you a challenge, the other gives you a rule. Play along in your own world and drink accordingly.

How it works

  1. Every turn, spin both wheels. You get one challenge and one rule. One turn is one Minecraft day: a new turn starts when the day counter in the top left turns from 0 to 1, from 1 to 2, and so on.
  2. Both only last for that turn. When the next turn starts, they are over and you spin again.
  3. Cooperative: if anyone completes the challenge, everyone is safe. Competitive: everyone has to complete it themselves, and whoever doesn't takes the penalty.
  4. Challenges get harder as the game goes on so they are never way too easy or way too hard. Rules are totally random.
  5. Harder challenge, bigger penalty. Colors on the challenge wheel show it:
    Very easy: 8 second chug
    Easy to medium: finish your drink
    Hard: shotgun a drink
    Very hard: take a shot
  6. Rules are always just sips.
  7. Every challenge means "before the next turn starts." Finish it before the turn changes.
  8. Never pause the game for a broken rule. Keep playing while you sip. You can pause between turns to pay a failed challenge, and at the start of a turn to spin.
  9. The same rules apply to every player.
  10. Not possible in your game or map? Press "Can't do this here" and the challenge wheel spins again.
