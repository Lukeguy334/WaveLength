Hangman

One player sets a secret word. Everyone else guesses letters before the drawing is complete.

How to play

  • The current setter picks a secret word or phrase — type your own privately, or draw a random one from a category.
  • Everyone else takes turns guessing one letter at a time.
  • A correct letter reveals every spot it appears. A wrong letter adds a piece to the drawing.
  • Guess the whole word before 6 wrong letters and the guessers win. Miss 6 times and the setter wins.
  • The setter role rotates every round.
