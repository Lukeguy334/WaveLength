8-Ball Pool

Top-down 8-ball for two. Aim, set the power, and sink your group before your opponent does.

How to play

  • Aim: press and drag on the table (or click) and the cue points that way. The ◀ ▶ buttons nudge the angle in tiny steps. The short lines show where the cue ball will touch, where the object ball goes, and where the cue ball deflects.
  • Power: set it with the slider, then press Shoot (or hit Space / Enter).
  • The first player breaks with the cue ball placed behind the head line. The table stays open until someone legally pockets a ball; that player takes that group (solids 1-7 or stripes 9-15).
  • Keep shooting while you pocket one of your own balls. Otherwise the turn passes.
  • You must hit one of your own balls first, and then either pocket a ball or send any ball to a rail. Once your group is cleared, the 8-ball is your target.
  • Fouls give your opponent the cue ball in hand: they can place it anywhere. Drag the cue ball to place it. A foul is any of these: the cue ball goes in a pocket (a scratch), the cue ball hits nothing, the first ball it hits is not one of yours, or after the hit no ball is pocketed and none touches a rail.
  • Scratch: if the white ball drops in a pocket it comes back onto the table, but it is a foul and your opponent gets ball in hand. A scratch on the break works the same way. If you scratch while sinking the 8-ball, you lose.
  • The 8-ball: once your group is cleared you have to call a pocket before each shot at the 8: tap one of the dotted rings around the table. The 8-ball has to drop in that pocket to win. Pocketing it in a different pocket, pocketing it early, or fouling while you pocket it, loses the game. For every other ball, any pocket counts. If the 8-ball drops on the break it is re-spotted.
  • If you aim at a ball that is not one of yours, a red X appears on it and the guide shows no path. Hitting it first is a foul.
