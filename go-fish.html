Go Fish

Ask for a rank, collect books of four, and empty your hand first.

How to play

  • Each player starts with a hand of cards (7 with 2–3 players, 5 with 4 or more). The rest form the pond.
  • On your turn, ask one opponent for a specific rank you already hold at least one of (e.g. "got any Queens?").
  • If they have any, they hand over all of that rank and you go again. If not, they say "Go Fish" and you draw from the pond — if you draw the rank you asked for, you go again.
  • Whenever you collect all four of a rank, it becomes a book and comes off your hand.
  • The game ends when all 13 books have been claimed or the pond runs out and no one can move. Most books wins.
