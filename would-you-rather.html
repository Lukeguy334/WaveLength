Would You Rather

How to play

The idea

Two options show up. Everyone privately picks the one they'd rather have happen, then the group's picks are revealed together.

Scoring

Landing with the majority earns a point. There's no wrong answer — it's just for laughs and to see who thinks alike.

An even split doesn't award anyone a point that round.

Categories

Pick a category (Funny, Weird, Food, Difficult, Gross, Family-friendly, Spicy/absurd) or mix them all together.

Modes

Play locally passing one device around (everyone answers privately before passing on), or host/join an online room where everyone answers on their own device.
