Balderdash

A truly obscure word appears. Bluff a believable fake definition, then vote for the real one.

How to play

  • Each round shows one player (the "reader") a genuinely obscure real word.
  • Everyone else privately writes a fake definition that sounds convincing.
  • All the fake definitions, plus the real one, get shuffled together and shown to the group.
  • Everyone (except the reader, who already knows the answer) votes for the definition they think is real — you can't vote for your own.
  • Scoring: writing a fake definition that fools someone earns you a point per vote it gets. Correctly spotting the real definition earns you 2 points. If nobody spots it, the reader gets a 2-point bonus.
  • The reader role rotates each round.
