Wheel of Fortune

Guess letters to fill the board, or solve the puzzle outright. No wheel spinning — just the puzzle.

How to play

Goal

Guess the hidden word or phrase before your opponent does, by calling out letters or attempting to solve the whole thing.

Playing

  • On your turn, pick any letter that hasn't been guessed yet.
  • If it's in the puzzle, every occurrence is revealed and you score points for it — and you go again.
  • If it's not in the puzzle, your turn ends and play passes to the next player.
  • Instead of guessing a letter, you can attempt to solve the whole puzzle at any time. Get it right and you win the round; get it wrong and your turn passes.

Scoring

  • Each consonant you reveal is worth 100 points per occurrence on the board.
  • Each vowel (A, E, I, O, U) is worth 50 points per occurrence.
  • Solving the puzzle outright adds a 350 point bonus on top of whatever you've already revealed this round.
  • Points you reveal only count if you're the one who ends up solving that round — if someone else solves it, your partial reveals for that round don't carry over.

Modes

Play solo just to solve puzzles for fun, pass-and-play locally with 2–4 people sharing one screen, or host/join an online room.
