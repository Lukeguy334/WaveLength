Would You Press the Button?

How to play

The idea

A button offers you a benefit — with a catch. Everyone privately decides Press or Don't Press, then the group's answers are revealed together.

Scoring

Landing with the majority earns a point. There's no wrong answer, just a fun way to see who's got the same risk tolerance.

An even split doesn't award anyone a point that round.

Modes

Play locally passing one device around (everyone answers privately before passing on), or host/join an online room where everyone answers on their own device.
