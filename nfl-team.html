How to play

Goal

Draft a 7-player lineup — QB, RB, WR, WR, TE, Base Defense, and a Wildcard Defensive Star — without going over a $25 budget. Then compare builds with whoever else drafted.

How drafting works

  • You draft one position at a time, in a random order, and you don't know what position comes next until you get there.
  • Base Defense and the Wildcard Defensive Star are always the last two picks of the draft, in that order.
  • Each round shows five options for that position, one from each price tier ($1 through $5). Pick one.

Legend tier

Every so often — roughly once a game — a Legend-tier option shows up for $7 (or $8 for a legendary defense), pulling from a list of all-time greats. If you see one, it's your call whether to splurge.

Hidden pricing

Once in a while, a $1–3 tier player is secretly priced a dollar higher or lower than normal — a steal, or a rip-off. There's no visual hint when this happens; you just have to go with your gut.

Budget

Going over $25 is allowed — the draft won't stop you — but your final comparison card will show it plainly.

Modes

Play solo just to build a team for fun, pass one device around locally, or host/join an online room where everyone drafts at the same time.

Keeping it current

The player lists (including the Legend tiers) are a snapshot and real rosters change every season — treat names and prices as flavor for the drafting game, not a factual roster.
