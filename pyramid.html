$100,000 Pyramid

Give clues, get your partner to name everything on the list before time runs out.

How to play

Goal

The clue-giver sees a category and a list of 6 answers. Using spoken clues — never the answer itself or a "rhymes with" hint — they try to get their partner (and anyone else listening) to say all 6 before time runs out.

Playing

  • Pass the device to the clue-giver. They see the category and all 6 answers privately, and keep holding the device (facing themselves) for the whole round — the guesser never looks at the screen.
  • Once the timer starts, they give one clue at a time for any answer on the list, in any order.
  • The moment someone guesses one correctly, the clue-giver taps that item to mark it off and moves to the next.
  • Stuck on one? Skip it and come back — nothing has to go in order.
Some categories are themed (like "Things a shower curtain would say") — the answers are creative phrases that fit the theme, not literal facts. Play it loose and have fun with the clues.

Scoring

Each item you get before time runs out is worth a point. Whoever's been clue-giver rotates each round, and scores add up across rounds.

Modes

Play solo to practice giving clues against the clock, pass-and-play locally with 2–8 people, or host/join an online room.
