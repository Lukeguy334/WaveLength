$100,000 Pyramid

Climb the pyramid one secret word at a time, then take on the Winner's Circle.

How to play

Goal

Six rounds, six boxes of the pyramid, bottom to top. Each round the clue-giver sees ONE secret word or phrase and has to get their partner to say that exact word before time runs out — using clues, never the word itself.

Difficulty climbs with the pyramid

The bottom row (rounds 1–3) is easy, literal, everyday stuff. The middle row (rounds 4–5) gets more abstract — verbs, specific descriptions. The top box (round 6) is the hardest: rare, abstract, or oddly-specific words. You'll watch the pyramid visually fill in as you go.

Main Game clue rules

  • Descriptive clues are allowed. Full sentences, prepositions, scene-setting — all fine. ("It's a grey animal with a trunk" works.)
  • Never say the secret word itself or any part of it, rhyme with it, invent a fake word, say a key word from it, use a synonym of it, or use hand gestures.

The Winner's Circle

Clear all 6 boxes' worth of rounds and you move on to a bonus round modeled on the show's Winner's Circle, with a rules reminder before it starts. This time the giver privately sees a category and its example items, and has to get their partner to name the category itself — by rattling off examples from it.

Winner's Circle clue rules are stricter: clues must be list-only — just naming items in the category, no descriptive sentences. Prepositions like "of" or "with" are banned here (they're fine in the Main Game). Every other ban still applies: no saying the answer or part of it, no rhymes, no invented words, no key words or synonyms, no gestures.

Scoring

Boxes pay out like the show: $1,000 on the bottom row, $5,000 in the middle, $10,000 for the top box. Clear the whole Winner's Circle before time runs out and you win the $100,000 grand prize.

Modes

Play solo to practice giving clues against the clock, pass-and-play locally with 2–8 people, or host/join an online room. With more than 2 players, the clue-giver rotates every round; whoever's leading after round 6 gets the Winner's Circle.

Winner's Circle rules

You've cleared the pyramid! Before the bonus round starts, remember: this round plays differently.

  • List-only clues. The giver can only rattle off items belonging to the category — no descriptive sentences.
  • Prepositions are banned ("of", "with", etc.) — only in this round, not the main game.
  • All the usual bans still apply: no saying the answer or part of it, no rhymes, no invented words, no synonyms, no gestures.
  • Guess the category itself from the examples — not an individual item.
One continuous clock. Get through as many categories as you can — skip a tough one and come back if there's time.
