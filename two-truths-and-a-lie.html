Two Truths and a Lie

Three surprising facts appear — two are true, one is made up. Can you spot the lie?

How to play

  • Three statements appear. Two are genuinely true facts, and one is fabricated.
  • Everyone votes for the statement they think is the lie.
  • Guess correctly and you score a point. The lie is revealed after everyone votes.
  • Play through as many rounds as you like — whoever has the most correct guesses at the end wins.
