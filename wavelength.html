How to play

Goal

Guess where the hidden target sits on the dial, using only a one-word clue from your team's psychic. The closer your guess, the more points you score.

Each round

  • A spectrum appears with two opposite ideas (e.g. "Overrated ↔ Underrated").
  • The psychic sees a hidden target zone on the dial and a secret number, then gives one word or short phrase meant to describe where on that spectrum the target sits.
  • Everyone else drags the needle to where they think the target is, based only on that clue, then locks in the guess.
  • Scoring is based on how close the needle lands to the true target — dead center scores the most, and points taper off toward the edges of the target zone.

Playing locally

Pass the device to whoever's the psychic. They note the target, then hide the screen (or hand the device to the middle of the table) before saying their clue out loud. Once the clue's given, tap "Start guessing" — the target disappears from view so the rest of the group can guess fairly.

Playing online

Host a room and share the code, or join with one. The psychic role rotates automatically each round.
