Imposter

How to play

Setup

Everyone gets shown the same secret word, from a category everyone can see — except one random player, the Imposter, who gets no word at all.

Giving clues

  • Going around in order, each player says one word or short clue related to the secret — without saying the word itself.
  • Non-imposters want to prove they know the word without making it obvious to the Imposter.
  • The Imposter doesn't know the word, so they have to bluff a plausible-sounding clue and hope nobody notices.

Voting

After clues are given, everyone privately votes for who they think the Imposter is. Votes are tallied and the most-voted player is revealed.

Winning

  • If the group correctly votes out the Imposter, the group wins.
  • If the Imposter escapes suspicion (or the vote ties), the Imposter wins.
  • Even if caught, the Imposter gets one last chance: if they can correctly guess the secret word, they steal the win anyway.

Modes

Best with 3–10 players. Play locally passing one device around, or host/join an online room where clues are typed instead of spoken.
