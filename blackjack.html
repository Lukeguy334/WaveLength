Blackjack

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on 17.

How to play

  • Each player is dealt two cards; the dealer gets one face up and one face down.
  • Number cards count as their value, face cards count as 10, and an Ace counts as 11 or 1 — whichever helps your hand most.
  • On your turn, Hit to take another card, Stand to hold, Double Down to double your bet and take exactly one more card, or Split if your first two cards match.
  • Go over 21 and you bust, losing your bet immediately.
  • Once everyone has acted, the dealer reveals their hidden card and hits until reaching 17 or more.
  • Beat the dealer's total without busting to win 1:1 on your bet. A natural blackjack (Ace + 10-value card on the first two cards) pays 3:2. Tie with the dealer is a push — your bet is returned.
