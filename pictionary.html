Pictionary

How to play

Goal

One player draws the secret prompt while everyone else tries to guess it before time runs out.

Each round

  • The drawer privately sees the word, then everyone gets a moment to talk and settle in before the clock starts — no pressure, chat as long as you need.
  • Once the timer starts, the drawer draws (no letters, numbers, or spoken hints) while everyone else calls out guesses.
  • Whoever's judging the round marks it "Someone got it!" the moment a guess lands, or reveals the word if time runs out first.
  • The drawer scores points for a correct guess; so does whoever guessed it (in local play, everyone who was paying attention shares credit).
House rule for tricky prompts: if the word or phrase is an idiom or a turn of phrase (something like "spill the beans" or "break the ice"), the drawer is allowed to say so before drawing — e.g. "this is a common phrase." That's fair game and doesn't count as cheating.

Charades mode

Same prompt list, no drawing — act it out instead. Switch to it from the setup screen before you start.

Difficulty & timing

Prompts are split into Easy, Medium, and Hard (or Mixed, drawing from all three). Turn on "+30s for hard words" in setup if your group's Hard list runs brutal.

Modes

Play locally passing one device around, or host/join an online room where the canvas syncs live and guesses are typed instead of shouted.
