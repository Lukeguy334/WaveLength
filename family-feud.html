Family Feud

Guess the top answers on the board. Three strikes and the other side can steal.

How to play

The questions and answers here are written to feel like typical survey results, but they're not from a real survey — there's no live audience being polled. Treat them as fun trivia-flavored prompts, not real statistics.

Main game

  • A question is shown with a hidden board of ranked answers.
  • The player in control types a guess, then passes the device to the other player to judge it against the real hidden board — a real person decides if it's close enough, so "broken" can reasonably count for "it was defective."
  • A match reveals that answer's spot and points, and the same player keeps guessing. No match is a strike.
  • After 3 strikes, if any answers are still hidden, control passes to the other player for one single steal attempt, judged the same way — get it right and they take all the points on the board; get it wrong and the original player banks the points instead.

Fast Money

  • One player answers 5 questions as fast as they can, one word or phrase per question.
  • A second player answers the same 5 questions afterward — but any answer that repeats player one's exact answer scores zero.
  • After both have answered, the group reveals each question's board together and marks which answer (if any) each response matches.
  • Add up both players' totals — 200 combined points is the classic target to "win the game."

Modes

Play the main game solo, locally with 2+ people, or host/join online. Fast Money is pass-and-play (solo or with a partner) since it's inherently sequential.
