20 Questions

Think of a person, place, or thing. Everyone else has 20 yes-or-no questions to figure out what it is.

How to play

  • The current answerer secretly thinks of a person, place, or thing (out loud in your head, or typed privately here if you want a reveal at the end).
  • Everyone else takes turns asking yes-or-no questions out loud. The answerer taps Yes, No, or Sometimes/Depends after each one — that's what counts toward the limit of 20.
  • Anyone can guess the secret at any point instead of asking a question — a wrong guess still uses up one of the 20.
  • Guess correctly before the 20 run out and you win the round. If the 20 run out first, the answerer wins.
  • The answerer role rotates every round.
