Jeopardy

Pick a category and value, buzz in first, answer before anyone else. Watch out for the Daily Double.

How to play

The board

  • 5 categories, 5 clues each, worth 200 to 1000 points. Whoever's "in control" picks the next clue.
  • Once a clue is revealed, buzz in first to get a shot at answering — only the first person to buzz gets to answer.
  • Answers are graded automatically, but anyone can hit the override button if the grader gets a close call wrong.
  • Correct answers add the clue's value to your score and hand you control of the board. Wrong answers subtract the value and lock you out of that clue — the buzzer reopens for everyone else.
  • If nobody gets it, the answer is revealed and everyone moves on.

Daily Double

  • One clue on the board is a hidden Daily Double. Whoever picks it, wagers, and answers alone — no buzzing.
  • Wager at least 5 points, up to your entire current score.

Final Jeopardy

  • Once the board is cleared, everyone secretly wagers on one last category, then answers the same clue.
  • Answers and wagers are revealed together at the end — the swings can be dramatic.
Traditionally you're supposed to phrase your answer as a question. We don't require that here — just get the content right.
